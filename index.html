About the Author

Meet Lynn Silver

Lynn Silver is a visionary author whose writing explores the extraordinary potential that lies within every human soul. With a gift for crafting compelling characters and immersive narratives, she has established herself as a powerful voice in contemporary supernatural fiction.

Her debut novel, Powers Unleashed, invites readers into a world where the boundaries between the ordinary and the supernatural dissolve — and where the greatest power of all lies in knowing yourself.

Through her writing, Lynn hopes to remind every reader that they are never alone on their journey, and that within each of us lives an untapped reservoir of strength waiting to be discovered.
